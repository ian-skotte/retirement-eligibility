Reference notes built into this tool

State, Higher Education and Local Education service rule

For State, Higher Education and participating Local Education retirees, creditable service may include State, Higher Education and participating Local Education service. Unused sick leave may also count where applicable.

Local Government service rule

For Local Government retirees, the agency must have opted in to offer retiree group health coverage. Local Government service cannot be combined with State, Higher Education, Local Education or another Local Government agency for group health eligibility.

Tennessee Plan reminder

The Tennessee Plan is supplemental medical insurance for retirees with Medicare. Applicants generally must be enrolled in at least Medicare Part A and must have an initial qualifying hire date before July 1, 2015. The Tennessee Plan does not coordinate with Medicare Advantage and does not include prescription drug coverage.

Dental and vision reminder

Retiree dental and vision continuation is not automatic. Eligibility depends on retirement status, agency participation and plan-specific rules. Vision generally also requires enrollment in a State-sponsored health insurance program.

Dependent documentation quick guide
  • Spouse: marriage certificate/license or naturalization papers showing marital status. If married more than 12 months, also provide proof of joint ownership, such as a recent bank statement, mortgage statement, lease, credit card statement, property tax statement or federal tax return page.
  • Natural child: birth certificate or equivalent birth record.
  • Adopted child: final adoption order, international adoption papers or court order placing the child for adoption.
  • Stepchild: marriage verification plus the child’s birth certificate showing relationship to the spouse.
  • Guardianship/custody/conservatorship: valid court order and required attestation.
  • Incapacitated dependent: Certificate of Incapacitation submitted before age 26, plus carrier review/approval.
