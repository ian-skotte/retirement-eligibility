Need help understanding the words?

Common words in plain language
  • Retiree insurance: Health insurance you may be able to keep after your job ends because you retired.
  • Continuous coverage: You were enrolled in insurance right before your job ended.
  • TCRS pension check: A monthly retirement payment from TCRS based on your own work.
  • Creditable service: The years of work that count toward retiree insurance.
  • Application timing: The deadline to turn in the retirement insurance form.
What papers may be needed?
  • Application to Continue Insurance at Retirement
  • Retirement date or TCRS notice
  • Proof of years of service
  • Proof of insurance coverage before your job ended
  • Proof that your agency offers the coverage, if you worked for Local Education or Local Government
Adding a spouse or child?
  • Spouse: marriage certificate. If married more than 12 months, proof of joint ownership may also be needed.
  • Child: birth certificate, adoption papers or court order, depending on the relationship.
  • Disabled dependent: special paperwork must be submitted before the child turns 26.
Important reminders
  • People hired on or after July 1, 2015 are generally not eligible to continue retiree group health coverage.
  • Local Government agencies must choose, in writing, to offer retiree health coverage.
  • Local Government service usually cannot be combined with service from another employer.
  • This tool is only a guide. Benefits Administration and the plan documents make the final decision.
